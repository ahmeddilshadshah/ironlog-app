Built for the way you train

Everything you need to log, follow and show your training, and nothing you don't.

  • Log in seconds

    Sets, reps, weight, duration and bodyweight exercises with a tap. The pickers start from your last numbers, and a rest timer is built in.

  • Your program, your way

    Create your own training days and exercises, pair them into supersets, and use the built-in exercise library or add your own.

  • See real progress

    Personal records, weekly trends and volume charts, plus body weight and measurements over time, all in one calendar history.

  • Share cards

    Turn any finished workout into a card with your photo, colours, font and layout, in an Instagram Story style, ready to share or save.

  • Private by design

    Your data stays on your device. No account, login or cloud needed, with one-file backup and restore and an export of your workout log.

  • Made for everyone

    Available in 15 languages, with full right-to-left support for Arabic and Urdu.
